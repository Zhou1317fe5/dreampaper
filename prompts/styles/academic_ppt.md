Academic Template Selection Rules:

Template A: Use for cover pages, chapter entry pages, achievement summaries, and key conclusion pages. Best when the page can be expressed as one central conclusion plus three supporting modules.

Template B: Use for technical body pages, method explanations, research route pages, and experiment design pages. Best when content naturally splits into three modules and benefits from a bottom "关键科学问题 / 核心问题" closure.

Template C: Use when A and B do not fit. Choose C-1 for 2/3/4 equal columns, C-2 for horizontal pipelines, C-3 for matrices, C-4 for left-text/right-visual or reverse, C-5 for top conclusion plus bottom grid, C-6 for center visual with satellite cards, and C-7 for combined layouts.

Always preserve the uploaded template's master elements first: title region, divider lines, page number, logo, safe margins, palette, and typography hierarchy.

Prompt style:
- 3840x2160, 16:9 by default.
- Mostly English implementation instructions with Simplified Chinese visible text.
- Use precise geometry, font sizes, colors, chart axes, labels, and module definitions.
- Do not add extra page numbers, footer citations, emoji, cartoon styling, random gradients, or decorative noise.
